# Browse

Browse is your full catalog — every song, exercise, and study material you have, not just what's suggested for today's session. Use it to look something up or fix a typo without needing it to appear on the dashboard first.

## Opening Browse

From the main practice screen, click **Browse** in the header.

## Finding something

1. Pick a tab: **Songs**, **Study Materials**, or **Exercises**.
2. Type into the search box to filter by name (for songs, this also matches the artist). Search is debounced, so results update shortly after you stop typing.
3. If there are more results than fit on screen, click **Load more** at the bottom to fetch the next page.

## Editing an entry

Every row has an edit (pencil) icon. Click it to open the same edit form used elsewhere in the app:

- **Songs** — name, artist, tuning, length, BPM, has-lead/has-singing flags, rhythm/lead/singing difficulty, date learned, and resources (links, YouTube, local files/folders, Guitar Pro files).
- **Exercises** and **Study Materials** — open their own edit forms with the fields relevant to that type.

Changes save immediately and reflect in the row right away — there's no separate "save and refresh" step needed.

## Quick recap

- Browse lists your whole catalog across three tabs: Songs, Study Materials, Exercises.
- Search narrows by name (and artist, for songs); **Load more** paginates.
- The pencil icon on any row opens an edit form for that item.
