# Metronome

A simple standalone click track you can pull up alongside anything you're practicing — it doesn't interact with item timers or sessions, it's just there to keep time.

## Opening the metronome

Click **Metronome** in the header. A small panel appears; click it again (or the ✕ in the panel) to close it.

## Setting the tempo

- Type a BPM directly into the number field (40–260).
- Or click **Tap** a few times in rhythm — it averages your taps and sets the BPM for you.

## Time signature

The dropdown next to BPM sets how many beats per bar: 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 6/8, or **No accent** (every beat clicks the same). With a time signature selected, beat 1 of each bar gets a louder, higher-pitched click so you can hear the downbeat.

## Starting and stopping

Click **Start** to begin the click track; the beat indicator flashes in time. Click **Stop** (same button, now labeled Stop) to end it. Changing the BPM or time signature while it's running restarts the click smoothly at the new setting — no need to stop first.

## Quick recap

- Set BPM by typing it in or tapping the **Tap** button.
- Choose a time signature to hear an accented downbeat, or **No accent** for a flat click.
- **Start**/**Stop** toggles the click; changes while running apply immediately.
